Acute ÷ chronic load (ATL ÷ CTL): this week's training load against what your body is used to. The shaded band (0.8–1.3) is where a steady training week usually sits; taper, down and rest weeks sit below it by design. The engine sizes every planned week against 1.25 — but on a shape-neutral reading of the ratio, not the raw sample shown here, which reads higher and may print above 1.30 with nothing breached; the governed number is published beside it. Race week is the deliberate outlier: it carries the race itself, which is fixed. It is a load ratio, not an injury predictor — the long-run-jump and fast-load brakes watch that axis.
